About the project

Why Pyaaz Nirikshan?

Manual inspection can vary from person to person. This prototype explores how a visual-first interface could make onion quality assessment faster, more consistent and easier to document.

The current analyzer is a front-end demonstration. Its score is generated locally and should not be treated as laboratory testing or food-safety certification.
